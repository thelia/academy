Thelia is developed and maintained by [OpenStudio](https://www.openstudio.fr). This academy is written by the same team, from the software's code and screens.

## Free software, on your own servers

- Thelia is released under the free GPL v3 license. The code is public: you can read it and modify it.
- The software costs nothing: no license fee.
- The store runs on the server of your choice. Your customer and order data stay with you.

## AI, with the provider you choose

The AI features of the Thelia core go through a connection you set up in the back office. You plug in the provider you want:

- French services: Mistral, Scaleway, OVHcloud, or Albert, the French State's AI service;
- a model installed on your own servers, with Ollama;
- OpenAI or Anthropic, if you choose to.

## Getting support

OpenStudio supports the merchants and agencies working with Thelia:

- setting up and extending the store;
- training teams, based on this academy;
- choosing sovereign hosting and AI.

[Contact OpenStudio](https://www.openstudio.fr)
